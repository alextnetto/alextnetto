I spent two months in Silicon Valley with a list of questions about the AI frontier. What can the models really do today? How are the fastest-growing startups building? Where is the money going? What can nobody answer yet?

This talk is an account of what I found in conversations with founders, at startups and at hackathons. I separate what I observed from what is my own interpretation, and translate it for people making decisions at their companies.

### Who it is for

- Business owners and executives who want a first-hand read on the frontier, without relying on headlines.
- Innovation, technology and strategy teams.
- Founders and investors outside the Valley.

### What the audience takes away

- A dated snapshot of the frontier, with observed facts kept apart from opinion.
- How to tell what already runs in production from what is still a demo.
- A concrete starting point to apply at their own company.

### Outline

1. The questions I brought and why they matter outside the Valley.
2. How frontier startups work: team size, everyday use of agents, delivery speed.
3. What is product and what is demo.
4. Where the money is going: the theses founders and investors are chasing.
5. The questions that are still open.
6. What I brought back: what changed in how I build, and what I would do at a company today.
